Welcome to the smart city information portal

ASmart City Information Portal is a digital platform designed to connect citizens, government departments, and urban services through a single online system. It provides real-time information about transportation, healthcare, education, public safety, utilities, and government services. By using advanced technologies such as the Internet of Things (IoT), cloud computing, and data analytics, the portal helps city administrators manage resources efficiently while improving the quality of life for residents. Citizens can access important updates, pay bills, register complaints, track public transport, and receive emergency alerts through the portal.
